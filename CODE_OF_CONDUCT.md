# Code of conduct

This archive is for optical schemes. Be respectful in pull requests, reviews, and comments.

## Expected

- Credit other people's work. Do not submit someone else's scheme as your own.
- Keep discussion on the scheme and the files in the PR.
- No harassment, hate, threats, doxxing, or spam.

## Reporting

Issues are disabled here. Report conduct problems on [loomrume/lantern](https://github.com/loomrume/lantern) or with GitHub's **Report content** on the organization.

Maintainers may close PRs, hide comments, or block accounts that break this.

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
